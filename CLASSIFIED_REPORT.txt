EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

This report addresses critical contractor compliance and oversight challenges 
involving Stark Industries and its Chief Executive Officer, Mr. Anthony Stark, 
across 17 active Department of Defense contracts totaling $47.3 billion.

KEY FINDINGS:

• Stark Industries consistently delivers technically superior products ahead 
  of schedule and under budget (a phrase we never thought we'd write about a 
  defense contractor)

• Mr. Stark's personal conduct has created unprecedented security, safety, and 
  diplomatic challenges over the past 90 days

• Traditional contractor oversight mechanisms are ineffective when the 
  contractor can fly away from meetings

• Multiple critical weapons systems are now dependent on Stark-proprietary 
  technology with Mr. Stark as the sole source of expertise

• Standard remedies (contract termination, debarment) would harm national 
  defense more than help it

RECOMMENDATION:

Congressional oversight and intervention is required to establish special 
compliance frameworks for strategic individual contractors who possess unique 
mission-critical capabilities.
